# adult with the Master Sword equipped, Kakariko by day (KakarikoVillage_Adult, Kakariko_WellSide), as adult-day.
# PASS (shots, both games): a-sheathed / a-drawn: A's own grip red (the game's own is teal-blue); B's shots show A's
# puppet's grip red and B's own grip green; A's shots show B's puppet's grip green. Logs: 'coop: gear params:
# Weapon_Sword_Master wears Mt_Weapon_Sword_Master.ns_param_color0' (own) and '... Weapon_Sword_Master_Blade<n> wears ...'
# or '_Look<k> wears ...' (the puppet's copy). FAIL: a grip in the game's own colour, or '... has no ...' in a log.
#! timeout 360
mark start

mark after
wait 600                # B lands too

press ZL                # camera behind A, close
wait 30

wait 40
mark a-drawn
wait 120
mark a-drawn-still
stick 1 0 20            # turn: the grip in the hand from the side
wait 30
mark a-drawn-side
wait 300                # B's turn to show its own (A stands still)
