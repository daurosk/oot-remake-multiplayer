# Both games load into Hyrule Field as adults; each game has its own Epona at -100.47 150.60 264.55
# (she is never a shared object). B (epona-own.B.txt) is the first rider; A watches from 10 m, then
# gets on its own Epona while B rides again.

# after make-puppet-pack in both kits (CoopRide_Epona). Pass lines (count them in the logs):
#   B min=1  coop: ride: we ride Animal_Epona_Adult                  (B got on)
#   A min=1  coop: ride: P\d+ rides Animal_Epona_Adult               (A draws B's horse)
#   A min=1  coop: spawn: CoopRide_Epona: create copy
#   A min=1  coop: quests: .*Get_Epona.*Complete                     (B's "Epona won" reached A)
#   A min=1  coop: ride: we ride Animal_Epona_Adult                  (A rides too, own horse)

#   both max=0  coop: ride: we ride \('.*but no Animal_Epona_Adult   (a horse we ride is always found)
# The ride's length is in "we got off, riding off sent (..., after N s)": a ride of under 2 s before
# the "owned" mark is the unowned Epona throwing Link off (the save has not won her yet); after B's
# setenum both games' quest Get_Epona is Complete.
# Shots: "mounted", "riding": B on a horse in A's window. "both-mounted", "both-riding": in each
# window the other player on a horse beside our own. "end": both on foot, no copy horse left.
# Longshot run 2026-11-13 15:02 (groups-20261113-145259): no mount: 'toward ... 6' gives the harness one push
# before it knows the camera's angle, so Link turned away from her (shot B-by-horse: Link at her tail, no
# Mount prompt). Now each rider is put 5 m off and walks to her side with goto, ending face to her. B's
# probe-pause shot must show the pause menu (else scripted buttons do not reach the game at all).
# Hookshot run 2026-11-13 (shots-hookshot/epona-own/20261113-012957): both adult at Epona with the
# 'Mount' prompt shown, but 'press A' (6 ticks) did not mount; after setenum Complete the field Epona
# is gone in both games (placed only while Get_Epona is not complete): the owned ride needs Epona's Song.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1
#! timeout 420
mark start
setflag Sequence_IsChildhood 0  # adult (Hookshot: run.ps1 loads the save first)
warp HyruleField_Adult HyruleField_FrontOfMarket
wait 1200               # the warp's load
mark field
place -100.5 151.0 254.5 # 10 m south of Epona
wait 240
toward -100.47 264.55 6  # face her (and B)
press ZL                 # camera behind A
wait 300                 # B's probe and walk to its Epona (goto: 1 to 3 s)
mark mounted             # B is on: a burst, as B's goto takes 1 to 3 s and an unowned Epona throws
wait 30                  #   her rider after about 1.5 s
mark mounted2
wait 30
mark mounted3
wait 30
mark mounted4
wait 30
mark mounted5
wait 30
mark mounted6
wait 80
mark riding
wait 490                 # B rides, stops, gets off
mark dismounted
wait 320                 # B waits by its horse
mark owned
place -105.5 151.0 264.5 # 5 m west of our own (unowned field) Epona
wait 240
goto -101.7 264.55 0.5 180 # walk to her side, ending face to her
wait 30
press A 12               # get on, as B does
wait 40
mark both-mounted
stick 0 0.8 230          # ride forward, both
mark both-riding
wait 120
press A 12               # get off
wait 240
mark end
wait 300                 # B's game says Epona is won; ours follows
mark won                 # the field Epona is gone in both games
