# hs-masks (Hookshot, issue 91): wear-masks-real on Hookshot. A is given three masks, puts each
# on ZR and wears it through the game's own equip call (`dress`), then takes the last one off; A steps aside to face B, B watches A's puppet.
# Nothing in the module differs from the emulator here (the same hook files; the pack is in
# mods\contents before Hookshot starts), so this run is the proof.
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-masks
# Pass (also: A's log 'test setenum PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.Child ?' lines: empty, Bomb, then Skull's own hash):
# - B's log: "P1 wears head 'Armor_Child_Mask_Skull'", then Bunny and Keaton; at off
#   "P1 takes off head ... (returned 0x1)", and no new "create CoopPuppet_Child".
# - B's shots b-skull, b-bunny, b-keaton: the mask on A's puppet; b-off: none.
# - A's shots: the mask on A's own Link at skull, bunny, keaton (after `dress head`); none at off.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1
mark start
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Skull.StockNum
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Bunny.StockNum
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Keaton.StockNum
wait 120
stick 0 0.6 100
stick 0.7 0 30
stick 0 -0.4 12
wait 60
mark before
# Readback (test-give-0116-2): 'setenum ... ?' logs the ZR enum's value as a hash. Bomb is the control (assign
# works for it): the value after 'assign Bomb' is Bomb's hash; after 'assign SkullMask' it must be a different
# non-zero hash, and after the press the item box must show the mask. Same hash as Bomb or unchanged = the
# game did not take the mask value (then compare the pause-screen shot and the ownership flags).
setenum PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.Child ?
give bombs
wait 30
assign Bomb
wait 30
setenum PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.Child ?
assign SkullMask
wait 30
setenum PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.Child ?
press ZR 10            # the item use, still tried as a player would (the item box shows the mask)
wait 120
setenum PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.Child ?
# The wear itself: the flag, then the game's own equip call (0x2df6c80 from the flags, as a save load and the
# pause menu make it): the mask is on A's own Link. 'wear' alone only wrote the flag.
wear head Armor_Child_Mask_Skull
wait 30
dress head
wait 240
mark skull
assign BunnyMask
wait 30
wear head Armor_Child_Mask_Bunny
wait 30
dress head             # the Bunny Hood in its place
wait 240
mark bunny
assign KeatonMask
wait 30
wear head Armor_Child_Mask_Keaton
wait 30
dress head
wait 240
mark keaton
wear head none         # taken off: the game's own take-off for an empty head flag
wait 30
dress head
wait 300
mark off
wait 300
mark end
