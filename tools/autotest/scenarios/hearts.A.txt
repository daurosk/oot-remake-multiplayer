# hearts: A gains a heart container (PlayerStatus.MaxLife + 4), a heart piece and a Gold
# Skulltula token through the test give (needs LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1); B stands still.
# With LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_FLAGS=1 B logs "our Link's life L of M, MaxLife flag F"
# (docs/items.md) and "our Link's max life 16 -> 20 (from MaxLife)" (on by default;
# LICHII_COOP_HEARTS_TO_LINK=0 on B shows the old behaviour: B writes 16 back).
mark start
wait 300
give PlayerStatus.MaxLife
wait 600
mark container
give PouchData.Obj_Heart_Piece.StockNum
wait 600
mark piece
give PouchData.Item_Token_Gold.StockNum
wait 600
mark token
press PLUS               # the menu shows hearts, pieces and tokens
wait 240
mark menu
press PLUS
wait 300
mark end
