# spawn-bomb (spawn sync, docs/spawns.md): A pulls a bomb and throws it; B, at
# the same save spot, should show the bomb copy and its explosion at the same
# time. Needs LICHII_COOP_OBJECTS_CHEAT=1 (bomb bag and bombs). Waits 15 s
# first so a slower B has finished loading the scene, then walks A a little to
# the right (away from the save spot's cow). R is quick select: it moves the
# item slot from the slingshot to the bombs.
mark start
wait 900
mark ready
stick 1 0 40
wait 20
mark turned
press R
wait 30
mark selected
press ZR
wait 45
mark pulled
press A
wait 20
mark thrown
wait 40
mark flying
wait 40
mark landed
wait 40
mark fuse
wait 40
mark exploded
wait 60
mark end
