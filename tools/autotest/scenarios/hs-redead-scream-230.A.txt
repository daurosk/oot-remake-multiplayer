# PASS (at least one round, no FAIL in the other):
#  1. The owner's game: "scream: our Enemy_ReDead froze our Link ('<anim>', N quarters): shared for the Links in its reach".
#  2. The other game: "scream: the owner's Enemy_ReDead froze its Link; ours is in its reach (X m): frozen here too".
#  3. Shots <x>-scream1 and <x>-frozen1 (~1 s later), BOTH windows: both Links in the paralysed pose on both screens,
#     the Redead in its scream pose.
# FAIL (row 230): 1 without 2 (or "out of its reach" with that Link inside 5.5 m in front), or on the shots one Link
#  moves freely while the other is frozen.
# VOID: no "scream:" line in either game (no Redead here, it never woke), or "no owner known" (co-op not on).

wait 300

toward -3.17 13.13 8

await 2400 scream: (our Enemy_ReDead froze our Link|the owner's Enemy_ReDead froze its Link)

wait 300
place -1.7 102.2 60.0
