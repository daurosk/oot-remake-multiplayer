# threads-of-time: opens the Minus menu, where Threads of Time lives (category 1 ChallengeLog, with the map
# and the Important page: docs/game-ui.md "the categories"; the game's own notice says "Open Threads of Time
# [M]"), and takes a shot of every page. Before big-run-0115 this opened the PLUS menu (Collection,
# Equipment, Items, System), which never holds the Threads of Time page, so no shot could show it.
# Pages: R twice to the right end, then L to the left end (no wrap), a shot on each step.
press MINUS
wait 240
mark tot-a-a-0
press R
wait 90
mark tot-a-a-1
press R
wait 90
mark tot-a-a-2
press L
wait 90
press L
wait 90
mark tot-a-a-3
press L
wait 90
mark tot-a-a-4
press MINUS             # close it
wait 120
press B                 # in case Minus did not close it
wait 120
