# hs-masks (Hookshot, issue 91): wear-masks-real on Hookshot. A is given three masks, puts each
# on ZR and wears it, then takes the last one off; A steps aside to face B, B watches A's puppet.
# Nothing in the module differs from the emulator here (the same hook files; the pack is in
# mods\contents before Hookshot starts), so this run is the proof.
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-masks
# Pass:
# - B's log: "P1 wears head 'Armor_Child_Mask_Skull'", then Bunny and Keaton; at off
#   "P1 takes off head ... (returned 0x1)", and no new "create CoopPuppet_Child".
# - B's shots b-skull, b-bunny, b-keaton: the mask on A's puppet; b-off: none.
# - A's shots: the mask on A's own Link at skull, bunny, keaton.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1
mark start
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Skull.StockNum
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Bunny.StockNum
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Keaton.StockNum
wait 120
stick 0 0.6 100
stick 0.7 0 30
stick 0 -0.4 12
wait 60
mark before
assign SkullMask
wait 30
press ZR 10            # puts the Skull Mask on
wait 240
mark skull
assign BunnyMask
wait 30
press ZR 10            # the Bunny Hood in its place
wait 240
mark bunny
assign KeatonMask
wait 30
press ZR 10
wait 240
mark keaton
press ZR 10            # the same mask again: taken off
wait 300
mark off
wait 300
mark end
