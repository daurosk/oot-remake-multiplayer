# Read in B's log: the commands A plays ("P1 plays '...'") from "at-hole" to "through", and
# whether "P1 show -> hold (their cutscene)" appears (A's game runs the crawl as an event).
# In A's log: whether "P2 show -> hide (our cutscene)" appears. Screenshots: does B's
