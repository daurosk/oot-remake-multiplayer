# cutscene-snap (issue 194): after a cutscene the other player's Link must not snap. Both load into
# the Deku Tree and are put in the slingshot room as in deku-ladder. A shoots the eye: the ladder
# falls (A's own cutscene; in B the live event, B's cutscene). Then B walks through the room's
# door (a door cutscene in B, "their cutscene" in A) while A watches it.
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Scenario cutscene-snap
# The checks (counts.txt):
#  - The cases happened (min=1): B's puppet of A went hold or hide at the ladder and came back
#    to show (B's own event ended too, so the ladder pose writes did not stall it); A's puppet of
#    B went "show -> hold (their cutscene)" at the door and back. A FAIL of door-* alone: the
#    door ran no event in B (then only the ladder part was checked; say so).
#  - snap-A/B: the puppet's own place, read back from the game every frame, never stepped
#    0.75 m to 20 m in one frame ("P<n> snap <m> m (show|hold)"; warps do not count).
#  - jump-A/B: the place we were handed (before any easing) jumped at most twice: a held
#    puppet that follows their place gives no jump when the cutscene ends. Each jump is logged
#    "P<n> target jump <m> m (show|hold, eased|at once|warp)": read them when it fails.
#    Counted only after each game's own "mark placed": the test's `place` moves of both
#    Links (setup, 35-53 m handed over 3-5 packets) are target jumps by design and
#    can give 3-5 such lines per game, all before "placed".
#  - navi-snap-A/B: the same snap check for Navi.
# Read too: B "...FallingLadder... moved by another player" lines while B's event runs, and the
# shots: B's puppet in A's window walks to the door during B's door cutscene.
#! noload
#! start coop: test warp done
#! switches LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_FLAGS=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_OBJECTS=1,LICHII_COOP_LIVE_EVENTS=1,A:LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1
#! timeout 600
start A B
play A cutscene-snap.A.txt B cutscene-snap.B.txt
lines ladder-held-B B min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ show -> (hold|hide) \((their|our) cutscene\)
lines ladder-back-B B min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ (hold|hide) -> show
lines door-held-A A min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ show -> hold \(their cutscene\)
lines door-back-A A min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ hold -> show
lines snap-A A max=0 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ snap \S+ m \((show|hold)\)
lines snap-B B max=0 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ snap \S+ m \((show|hold)\)
lines jump-A A max=2 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ target jump \S+ m \((show|hold), (eased|at once)\)(?<=coop: test mark placed[\s\S]*)
lines jump-B B max=2 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ target jump \S+ m \((show|hold), (eased|at once)\)(?<=coop: test mark placed[\s\S]*)
lines navi-snap-A A max=0 fairy coop: puppet: P\d+ snap \S+ m \((show|hold)\)
lines navi-snap-B B max=0 fairy coop: puppet: P\d+ snap \S+ m \((show|hold)\)
