# hs-boss-handover-gohma (fable-g2-bosses, rows 104/118, #150): every player's hits count across Gohma's hand-overs.
# B walks in first and owns Gohma; A walks in ~25 s later with a copy. Then ten turns, 600 frames each, the same
# on both sides: turn 1 B (the owner) hits 3; turns 2-9 the player whose turn it is tells its copy's owner it is
# stunned (`stun`: the stun hand-over of issue 150, a DAMAGE of 0) and hits 2 (`hit`: through the game's own Life
# step; on a copy the fall goes to the owner as DAMAGE). 3 + 8 x 2 = 19 for turns 1-9, so turn 10 is a kill (a hit of 2 stops at her life 1, 'The life stops' in docs/dungeons-boss.md; the owner's kill is a kill, a copy's is held at 1 and goes to the owner): she dies on turn 10.
# Turns 2-5 hit 20 frames after the stun (inside the hand-over: H3 our unsent hit, H4 our DAMAGE on the wire);
# turns 6-10 hit 180 frames after it (the hand-over done: the hitter owns her, its "life L" must be 21 minus every
# hit before). Either game may own her at the start (the chase hand-over, hs-gohma-death): a `stun` from the owner
# logs "not a copy" and changes nothing; the sum is the same.
# SAVE: both lane baselines at quest Get_StoneOfForest = GoToBossRoom (as hs-gohma-death); the offline job
# boss-handover-probe.ps1 sets it on slots 00-02 and puts the saves back. The scenario clears the boss room flags.
# PASS (the job's verdict): every settled turn's (6-10) "coop: test hit Enemy_DungeonBoss_Gohma N 0x.. (.. m, life L)"
# has L = 21 - the sum of every hit logged before it (L higher = a hit given back to her across a hand-over, the
# bug; lower = a hit taken twice), and she dies in both games after turn 10 and not before. Notes counted:
# "ours now with a hit of ours not sent yet", "our own hit crossed the hand-over", the server's "given back across
# a hand-over N". A death that never comes while every L is right is INCONCLUSIVE (a life stop held her), not FAIL.
