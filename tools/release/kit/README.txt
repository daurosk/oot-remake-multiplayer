Squadala! (Longshot) - online co-op: play Ocarina of Time together, each player on their own PC with their own copy of the game.
1. Unzip this folder to a short place such as C:\Squadala (not inside the zip, not in OneDrive).
2. Double-click "Start Squadala.bat". The first time it asks for your game file (drag your .nup into the window)
   and builds the game from it: 10 to 45 minutes, about 60 GB free, once. After that it starts in seconds.
3. In the game, open the menu: F1, or - then R (on a controller - then R past Map), or - on the title screen.
   Its Multiplayer tab (right after Map): Host gives you a code for your friends; Join takes one.
Always start with "Start Squadala.bat", never longshot.exe: you do not need your own Longshot build (the kit builds its own game in hookshotgame, with co-op inside), and a game started by hand has no co-op or only part of it. It updates itself (Revert.bat goes back); every player needs the same version. Needs 64-bit Windows and a graphics card with mesh shaders.
Something wrong? The window says what to do. Others only names, no Link? make-puppet-pack.cmd makes the puppet pack again.
More help: docs\SETUP.md (NVIDIA RTX card? "NVIDIA DLSS.bat" adds DLSS). Bug report: scripts\collect-logs.cmd, then /bugreport on the Squadala! Discord (https://discord.gg/GwtRKAqng5) with its zip. Problem reports (masked; sent to the Squadala! team after a game with a problem only if you said yes): scripts\problem-report.cmd send, nosend, on or off (docs\problem-reports.md).
Nothing of the game is in this kit, and nothing of yours leaves your PC except the co-op traffic while you play and, only if you said yes, the masked problem reports (scripts\problem-report.cmd off stops them).
