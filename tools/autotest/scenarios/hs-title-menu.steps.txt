# hs-title-menu (Hookshot only): the title screen's own co-op rows (patch 0004 + the puppet pack's
# title_menu step, docs/hookshot.md "The title menu"). A starts as the harness starts it (co-op on, coop.ini
# names the test server) and stays at the title: Continue, New Game, Options, then Host, Join, Co-op
# Settings in the same list. A moves down four rows, presses A, then B twice.
# Needs the puppet pack built again with this branch WITH --title-rows (Build-Hookshot-Coop passes it; its log says
# "title menu: Host, Join and Co-op Settings on the title"), installed in A's game.
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-title-menu
# Pass: the lines below, and the shots (player-A.txt names them):
#   rows          six rows in the game's own look: Continue, New Game, Options, Host, Join, Co-op Settings
#                 (only three: the new parts do not show; three more but empty: the texts were not set)
#   third-down / fourth-down   the game's own highlight bar and ornaments on the row reached (the log's
#                 "focus is on button N" lines give the order)
#   pressed       if the row was one of ours: the game's own press look, then the co-op page (Host's status,
#                 Join's field or the settings) over the title; else whatever the game's row does
#   closed        the title again, its rows all there, the cursor usable (the game is not stuck)
# Sounds (heard only): the game's own move and press sounds.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1
#! timeout 240
start A
sleep 15
play A hs-title-menu.A.txt
lines menu-on A min=1 coop: title menu: on \(Minus at the title\)
lines title-seen A min=1 coop: title menu: the title screen 0x[0-9a-f]+, \d+ buttons
lines rows-set A min=1 coop: title menu: the title's own rows Host, Join, Co-op Settings \(buttons 4 to 6\)$
lines rows-no-text A max=0 their texts could not be set
lines focus-moves A min=3 coop: title menu: the title's focus is on button \d
lines no-sound-trouble A max=0 coop: title menu: the game's sound name
