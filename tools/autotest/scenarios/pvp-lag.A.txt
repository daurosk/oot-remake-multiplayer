# pvp-lag (emulator, under net-sim; no issue): pvp's sword hits under 150 ms lag and 2% loss.
#   tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Scenario pvp-lag -Lag 150 -Loss 2
# (Hookshot runs have no net-sim: run.ps1 refuses -Lag with -Runtime hookshot. Without -Lag this is a
# plain pvp run on either runtime.)
# Why a new file: in bigrun 2026-11-12 the lag runs showed only the /pvp toggle. First A was still on
# the loading screen when it swung (pvp\20261112-001013); then a copy with doubled waits sent A into
# Kakariko and swung at nothing. pvp itself only passed by luck: its `goto 213 14` is past the
# Kakariko gate from the field start (pvp\20261112-000323: both "mark field" at about 200.4 159.6 19.3,
# then A at -98 97.8 55 and B at -117 97.0 58 in Kakariko). Here both are PUT in Hyrule Field beside the
# start point with `place`, after a long load wait, and put again once more later (a slow load under
# lag must not make the first place miss: its log says "failed" then). No walking, so no gate.
# Pass (as pvp):
# - off1..off3: no "coop: pvp:" hit lines; B's life unchanged.
# - after "chat /pvp on": both "coop: pvp: on (set by 1)"; A "coop: pvp: our sword reached P2" for the
#   swings on1..on3; B "coop: pvp: hit from P1 (2 quarter hearts): taken" and "the game took the hit" or
#   "our Link's life N -> N-2", at most one taken per 0.8 s, under the lag.
# - off-again: no hit after "/pvp off".
# Check first: both "mark placed2 (Link at ~201 159.6 ~21)" (in the field, about 1.2 m apart).
#! noload
#! start coop: test warp done
#! switches LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_OBJECTS=1
#! timeout 420
mark start
warp HyruleField_Child HyruleField_FrontOfKakarikoVillage
wait 1200
mark title
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A                 # loads the save, then the warp
wait 3600               # a load under lag takes longer
mark field
place 201.4 159.8 21.0  # 1.2 m east of B's spot
wait 600
place 201.4 159.8 21.0  # once more, in case the load was still running at the first
wait 120
toward 200.2 21.0 10    # face B
mark placed2
wait 60
mark off-ready
hold Y 8
wait 60
mark off1
hold Y 8
wait 60
mark off2
hold Y 8
wait 90
mark off3
chat /pvp on
wait 240                # the toggle crosses the lag
mark on-ready
toward 200.2 21.0 6
hold Y 8
wait 30
mark on1
wait 30                 # more than the 0.8 s mercy
toward 200.2 21.0 6
hold Y 8
wait 30
mark on2
wait 30
toward 200.2 21.0 6
hold Y 8
wait 30
mark on3
wait 120
chat /pvp off
wait 240
hold Y 8
wait 60
mark off-again          # no hit after /pvp off
wait 120
mark end
