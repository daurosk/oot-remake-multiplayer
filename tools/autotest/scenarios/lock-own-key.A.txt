# lock-own-key (locked doors with two players at the door; docs/dungeon.md): both load into the
# Water Temple entrance. A gets one small key (setflag; the gain is shared, so B has 1 too), is put
# at the top floor's west door (locks 0x245948bc6d57c9e0 and 0xb2d8d82145ce4d26 at -20.83 119.30
# -4.50) and waits there while B comes with /teleport A from the entrance (another room of the
# dungeon). A opens the lock with its key and walks through; B, standing at its own still-shut
# copy of the lock, opens it with its OWN key and walks through (lock-own-key.B.txt).
# Pass (LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_FLAGS=1 logs every count change as "int 0x877bdcee ... N -> M item
# DungeonInfo.WaterTemple.SmallKeyNum"):
#   A: "0 -> 1" after setflag, then "1 -> 0" when it opens; "world: ... Obj_DoorChainLock_A_01
#      broken here (gone next to Link): sent"; a-through with x under -22.
#   B: "0 -> 1" (the shared gain), "teleport: next to A" and b-teleported at about -20.1 119.3 -4.5
#      (the teleport into another room, picture not black), then "1 -> 0" when B opens its copy and
#      b-through with x under -22.
#   Neither log has "one key lent", "given back" or a count above 1; both end at 0.
# The steps turn Link to the door (toward) before every press: after a wait Link no longer faces
# it, and a goto into the shut door steps aside and leaves Link off the lock.
#! noload
#! start coop: test warp done
#! switches LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_FLAGS=1
#! timeout 420
mark start
warp WaterTemple WaterTemple_FromLakeHylia
wait 1200
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A
wait 2400
mark in
setflag DungeonInfo.WaterTemple.SmallKeyNum 1
wait 120
place -19.6 119.4 -4.5
wait 150
goto -20.4 -4.5 0.3 90     # up to the door (it stops Link at about -20.1)
wait 30
mark a-at-lock
wait 900                   # B comes with /teleport A
mark a-open
toward -21.5 -4.5 8
wait 20
press A 12
wait 60
toward -21.5 -4.5 8
wait 20
press A 12
wait 60
toward -21.5 -4.5 8
wait 20
press A 12
wait 60
toward -21.5 -4.5 8
wait 20
press A 12
wait 360
mark a-opened
goto -26.0 -4.5 1.5 300
wait 60
mark a-through
wait 2400
mark end
