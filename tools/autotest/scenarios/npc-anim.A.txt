#! switches LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_NPC=1
# npc-anim (issue 200): A runs an event in Kakariko (reads the signpost, as cutscene.A.txt) and
# holds the text open about 12 s; B stands a few metres away outside it (npc-anim.B.txt).
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Scenario npc-anim (the header adds LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_NPC=1).
# Expect in A's log while reading: "coop: npc-anim: our Link is in an event: N NPCs near it are ours now"
# (N > 0 when a townsperson is within 30 m), then "the event's NPCs are free again" ~2 s after the text closes.
# In B's log: "coop: npc-anim: <Npc_...> copied from P1 at x y z plays '<anim>' frame f" for those NPCs,
# once a second, all through "reading" (P1 = A; before the event the owner may be P2 = B itself, so
# those lines name P2 or are missing). Screenshots at "reading": the NPCs near the post stand and
# animate the same in both windows. N = 0 means no townsperson was near the post: then any NPC
# talk (A in front of an NPC, e.g. Mido or Saria in Kokiri Forest) is the next thing to try.
mark start
wait 120
stick 0 -1 120          # back down the path, out of the Graveyard
wait 420                # Kakariko loads
mark kakariko
stick 0 1 60
goto -5.9 70.5 0.6      # just east of the post
toward -7.46 71.12 8
mark at-post
wait 20
press A 12              # read it: the event starts
wait 240
mark reading
wait 360
mark reading-2
wait 120
press A 12              # through the text
wait 60
press A 12
wait 60
press B 12
wait 240
mark closed
