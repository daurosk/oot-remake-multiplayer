# wear-masks-real (issue 91): A really puts masks on, as a player does: the mask is given,
# put on ZR (`assign`) and used (ZR), then used again to take it off. `wear-masks` only
# set the flag, and the masks never showed on the puppet (chunk 5 shots): this run shows how
# the game itself wears one. A steps aside to face B; B watches A's puppet.
# Run with -Switches "LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1".
# Look at:
# - A's shots: the mask on A's own Link at skull, bunny, keaton; none at off.
# - A's log, "slots (local player" lines: which hand slot (slot N) or armor link (armor N)
#   holds the mask while it is worn. B's log, "P1 PLAYER_EQUIP": whether `head` names it.
# - B's log: "P1 wears head 'Armor_Child_Mask_Skull'"; at off "P1 takes off head ...
#   (returned 0x1)" and no new "create CoopPuppet_Child". B's shots: the mask on A's puppet,
#   then none.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1
mark start
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Skull.StockNum
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Bunny.StockNum
give PouchData.Armor_Child_Mask_Keaton.StockNum
wait 120
stick 0 0.6 100
stick 0.7 0 30
stick 0 -0.4 12
wait 60
mark before
assign SkullMask
wait 30
press ZR 10            # puts the Skull Mask on
wait 240
mark skull
assign BunnyMask
wait 30
press ZR 10            # the Bunny Hood in its place
wait 240
mark bunny
assign KeatonMask
wait 30
press ZR 10
wait 240
mark keaton
press ZR 10            # the same mask again: taken off
wait 300
mark off
wait 300
mark end
