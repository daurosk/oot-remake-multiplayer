OoT co-op (Hookshot) - play Ocarina of Time together, each player on their own PC with their own copy of the game.
1. Unzip this folder to a short place such as C:\OoT-Coop (not inside the zip, not in OneDrive).
2. Double-click "Start OoT Co-op.bat". The first time it asks for your game file (drag your .nup into the window)
   and builds the game from it: 10 to 45 minutes, about 60 GB free, once. After that it starts in seconds.
3. In the game: Host or Join on the title screen. Host gives you a code to send your friends; Join takes a friend's code.
It updates itself; every player needs the same version. Needs 64-bit Windows and a graphics card with mesh shaders.
Something wrong? The window says what to do. More help: docs\SETUP.md. Logs for a bug report: scripts\collect-logs.cmd.
Nothing of the game is in this kit, and nothing of yours leaves your PC except the co-op traffic while you play.
