# hs-fairy-revive, B: lands in Hyrule Field in front of Kakariko, puts a fairy in bottle A, then takes the game's own
# hits on its Link, a heart each (`hurt 4`), until the life is gone: the game's death uses the fairy
# (see hs-fairy-revive.A.txt). Stays where it lands (no walking).
mark start
warp HyruleField_Child HyruleField_FrontOfKakarikoVillage
wait 1200
mark title
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A
wait 2400
mark field
setflag PouchData.BottleA.StockNum 1
wait 30
setflag PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.BottleInfo.BottleA.IsValid 1
wait 30
setenum PlayerStatus.AssignableItem.BottleInfo.BottleA.BottleItem Fairy
wait 300                # A has turned down but not out off by now (its chat at its own "field" mark)
mark hurt-start
# A heart a second. The kit save has 12 quarter hearts (run 1: "life 12 of 16"), so the third hit is lethal and the
# game's death uses the fairy. Nothing more for 20 s (no hit lands during the revive), then two more hearts in case
# this save had more life (the revive measured is the first 0 -> above 0).
hurt 4
wait 60
hurt 4
wait 60
hurt 4
mark lethal
wait 1200
mark late-hurts
hurt 4
wait 60
hurt 4
wait 1200
mark end
