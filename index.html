oot-remake-multiplayer

Online co-op for the Ocarina of Time remake on PC. Two to four players see each other in the world, share progress and fight the same enemies, on the Lichii emulator or on the Hookshot PC version.

  • Download the test releaseThe newest test build is the top pre-release. You bring your own copy of the game.
  • Setup guideHookshot or the emulator kit, updating, settings and troubleshooting.
  • Play over the internetInvite codes: the host sends one code, friends paste it in.
  • Source code and issuesGPL-3.0. Bug reports and testing help welcome.
  • DiscordFor players, testers and contributors.
A fan project. No game files are included or distributed; each player needs their own copy of the game.
