# (B, cutscene-snap rule). Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Scenario cutscene
# Shot at "reading": A's window has no puppet or Navi of B; B's has A's puppet standing at the post.
# If both "hide" counts are 0 and "hold" too, reading a sign ran no event: INCONCLUSIVE, not FAIL.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_OBJECTS=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_OBJECTS=1
start A B
load A B
expect both-in A=1 B=1
play A cutscene.A.txt B cutscene.B.txt
lines our-hides-A A min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ show -> hide \(our cutscene\)
lines our-navi-hides-A A min=1 fairy coop: puppet: P\d+ show -> hide \(our cutscene\)
lines our-back-A A min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ hide -> show
lines their-holds-B B min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ show -> hold \(their cutscene\)
lines their-back-B B min=1 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ hold -> show
lines snap-B B max=0 (?<!fairy )coop: puppet: P\d+ snap \S+ m \((show|hold)\)
expect after A=1 B=1
