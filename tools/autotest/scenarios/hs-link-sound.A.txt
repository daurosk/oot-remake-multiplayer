# hs-link-sound (Hookshot, issues 131 and 152, branch ocarina-sound-b): the sounds the game starts on
# A's own Link reach B's puppet of A, from the game's own calls (docs/puppet.md "Link sounds").
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-link-sound (child save, Fairy Ocarina).
# Needs Hookshot built with patches 0005 and 0014 (A's log has no "no sound wrap (patch 0014)" line).
# The autotest has no audio: the proof is in the logs. A's log: "coop: sound probe: user 0x...
# (our Link) emits '...'" lines (what the game starts on A's Link: steps, swings, voice, the
# Ocarina's notes if they go there) and "coop: link sound '...' sent". B's log (DEBUG_ANIM):
# "P1 sound '...'" for the same names while A is near, none while A is far (mark far).
# B only waits.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_SOUND=1
mark start
wait 120
mark walk
stick 0 0.8 90
stick 0 -0.8 90
wait 30
mark sword
press Y 12
wait 40
press Y 12
wait 40
press Y 12
wait 60
mark roll
stick 0 0.8 10
press A 8
wait 60
press B 12
wait 60
mark ocarina-out
press DLEFT 12
wait 240
# The first Ocarina use may show an "Ocarina Audio Input" note; A closes it.
press A 12
wait 120
mark notes
hold L 30
wait 60
hold R 30
wait 60
hold Y 30
wait 60
hold X 30
wait 60
hold A 30
wait 90
press B 12
wait 120
press B 12
wait 90
mark far
# 45 m away: B hears nothing of A's steps (no "P1 sound" in B's log from here on)
stick 0 0.9 600
press Y 12
wait 60
mark end
