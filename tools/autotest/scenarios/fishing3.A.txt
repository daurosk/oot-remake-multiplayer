# fishing3 (#151, branch fishing-3): two players fish at once, each with their own catch.
# Both load into the pond (warp HyruleField_Child MiniGame_Fishing_PlayerStart), walk to the
# owner, talk and accept (A mashed through the talk: pay, take the rod), step apart, cast (B),
# wait for a bite, reel (A held) and wait again. B does the same 3 s later, a few metres to
# the right, so the two lures are apart.
# Expect (LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_OBJECTS=1):
#  - both logs: `share fish lure N fish` lines (every fish listed; N about 30).
#  - after a cast: `near our lure` above 0 in the caster's log; `share owner <fish> is player
#    <caster>` for fish near that lure.
#  - a bite: `<fish> ... hooked` in the caster's log only; that fish is never handed over
#    while hooked (no `share owner` line for it in either log until it is gone).
#  - a landed fish: `share caught here:` in the catcher's log, `share caught in its owner's
#    game:` in the other's, for the same fish; each player's own record/talk afterwards.
# Paying and casting are scripted blind (the talk's choices are guessed): look at the
# screenshots at marks a-rod, a-cast, a-reel; if no rod came, use the in-game checks of
# reports/fishing-3.md.
#! noload
#! start coop: test warp done
#! switches LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_OBJECTS=1,LICHII_COOP_OBJECTS_RELEASE=1
#! timeout 420
mark start
warp HyruleField_Child MiniGame_Fishing_PlayerStart
wait 1200               # the title screen
mark title
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A                 # loads the save, then the warp
wait 2400
mark a-in
stick 0 1 60            # to the owner's counter
wait 60
press A 12              # talk
wait 240
press A 12
wait 180
press A 12              # yes: pay
wait 180
press A 12
wait 180
press A 12
wait 180
press A 12
wait 240
mark a-rod
stick 0 -1 90           # back out to the water
wait 60
stick -1 0 40           # a little left of B
wait 60
press B 12              # cast
wait 600
mark a-cast
hold A 300              # reel in
mark a-reel
wait 600
press B 12              # cast again
wait 900
hold A 600
mark a-end
wait 600
mark end
