# ocarina-lazy, B's part: takes out its own Ocarina (DLEFT), closes the first-use note (A),
# plays one note (L) and puts it away (B B), before A's song starts.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1
mark b-start
wait 60
press DLEFT 12
wait 240
press A 12
wait 120
mark b-ready
hold L 30
wait 60
mark b-note
press B 12
wait 30
press B 12
wait 60
mark b-end
